Solar cycles exist. That doesn't establish 'the Sun, not humans' as the cause of modern warming. NASA reports no upward trend in incoming solar energy since satellite monitoring began in 1978. A fitted cycle isn't a validated climate forecast.

## Longer methodological response

```text
The issue isn't whether solar-proxy data exist. They do. It's whether the proposed cycles reliably explain modern warming and predict future cooling. Some studies find long-period signals; others challenge their significance or solar origin. A fitted curve is not a validated causal explanation. That needs a quantitative model tested against independent observations, with uncertainty and competing causes included.

NASA: https://science.nasa.gov/climate-change/faq/is-the-sun-causing-global-warming/
Usoskin (2023): https://doi.org/10.1007/s41116-023-00036-z
```

Evidence: [S01, S02, S03, S05, S06]. Do not claim all solar cycles are imaginary, no historical data exist, or a single irradiance plot eliminates all indirect mechanisms. Do not claim the whole book has been reviewed.
