1. Do not say there is no real proxy data or no peer-reviewed support for long-period solar signals. Older supportive results are explicitly retained [S05, S06, S08].
2. Scope the Eddy non-significance finding to the reconstruction and analysis discussed, rather than making it a universal theorem [S02, S05].
3. Describe the Hallstatt solar/geomagnetic interpretation as a substantive scientific disagreement; do not imply one new study proves all long-term solar variability impossible [S03, S04, S06].
4. Do not use the 2007 stochastic interpretation as the last word while ignoring the same researcher's later work [S07, S06].
5. The solar-energy record constrains increasing-irradiance attribution; it is not by itself a complete falsification of every lagged or indirect mechanism [S01, S10].
6. Radiocarbon complications after 1950 are genuine. Extrapolation is a prediction to test, not necessarily evidence of intentional data suppression [S03].
7. Separate Vinós's conditional account of reduced human influence from an advocate's stronger exclusion of human influence [S14].
8. No cover-to-cover book review, numerical replication, raw-data download, independent peer review or dated glacier forecast has been completed.
