# Archived report — August-to-July 2025–26

**Frozen:** 2 September 2026  
**Status:** Published-input calculation (complete)

This folder preserves the original Project 001 research report that asked whether **1 August 2025 to 31 July 2026** was the warmest equivalent August-to-July period in the Wales mean-temperature record.

## Headline result (archived)

| Measure | Result |
|---|---:|
| August 2025 to July 2026 mean | **10.61°C** |
| Rank among equivalent August-to-July periods | **1 of 142** |
| Rank among all monthly-start 12-month windows | **5** |
| July 2026 published input | **17.8°C** |
